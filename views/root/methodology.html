Methodology

How public records become plain-language summaries on the network.

Home About

Sources

Each state feed starts with legally available public records published by state and local agencies. We collect source documents, extract the factual core, and present it in a consistent format.

Summarization

Records are summarized into plain-language headlines and short articles. The goal is clarity and consistency across states, not editorial opinion.

Updates & corrections

When source agencies update or correct a record, we reflect the change on the feed. Readers can submit a correction at any time.

What do these scores mean?

  • Coverage — share of {{STATE_NAME}} counties that have at least one published report.
  • Freshness — share of published reports from the last 7 days.
  • Source diversity — variety of public-safety data sources feeding the pipeline.
  • Publishing velocity — average number of reports published per day.
  • Recency — how recently a new report was published.
